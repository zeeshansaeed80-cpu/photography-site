How licensing works

  1. Send an enquiry. Tell me which photo you'd like and how you plan to use it.
  2. Get a quote. I'll confirm the photo is available at full resolution and send you a price.
  3. Receive your files. Once paid, you get the full-resolution file and your written licence.

A note on availability

Some of my older photos survive only as the web-sized files you see on this site, because the originals were lost. Those can't be licensed or sold as downloads. I'll always confirm that a photo is available before you pay.
