Turkey

Istanbul, Antalya, Konya and Cappadocia

A sixteen-day family tour in the summer of 2019, and the oldest trip on this site. We started in Istanbul's Old City, flew south to Antalya and the Mediterranean coast, then crossed inland to Konya and on to Cappadocia's fairy chimneys and sunrise balloons, before ending back in Istanbul around Taksim and the Old City. The full story, day by day, is in the Journal: Sixteen days in Turkey. These are the photos, city by city.
